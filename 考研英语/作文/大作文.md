## 动态图 第一句 + 第二句
- 句① 动态单/多方向
- The chart above clearly depicts the trajectory of [主题词] from [年份] to [年份], throughout which it underwent a steady growth（上升）/ a gradual decline（下降）. (单方向)
	- with [单项] showing the most striking rise/fall. (多方向，方向不同)
	- 可选修饰词：steady、consistent、gradual、substantial、marked、rapid

- 句① 动态多方向 + 对比
- 一般只描述**两个变量**。
- As vividly demonstrated in the chart, [主题词] exhibited divergent trajectories between [年份] and [年份], with [单项] on a steady climb whereas [另一单项] suffered a continuous contraction.
	- divergent adj. 不同的
	- 如果两个变量**方向相同**，改几个词即可，例如divergent、whereas。


## 静态图 第一句 + 第二句
- 静态图无年份趋势，不用from...to...而是改为 in 年份；

- 句① 静态
- The (pie chart / table) above delineates the breakdown of [主题词] in [年份], with [单项] accounting for the lion' s share.
	- delineates the breakdown of **描绘了……的构成**
	- accounting for the lion' s share **占据了最大的一部分**

## 万能场景 第一句
- 句① 涉及变化(动态)
- As can be seen from the table above, recent years have witnessed profound shifts in [主题词].

- 句① 涉及比例/份额(静态)
- The chart above provides information about [主题词] from [年份] to [年份].
