## 必背单词、常用搭配
- 增加：increase / rise / grow / expand / climb / jump / surge / soar

- 减少：decrease / decline / diminish / shrink / drop / fall 

- 持平：remain flat / stay at / stand at / stabilize

- 波动：vary / rise and fall/ fluctuate

- 迅速地： sharply / markedly / notably / remarkably / significantly / dramatically

- 缓慢地： slightly / gradually / steadily / modestly / a modest rise

- 占： account for / make up / constitute

- 比例：proportion / percentage / share / ratio

- 峰值：peak at / reach a peak of / hit a record high / reach an all-time high

- 谷值：bottom out at / hit a trough / fall to a low

- 时间： between A and B / from A to B / during the period from A to B / throughout the past XX years

- 约数与修饰：approximately / roughly / nearly / just over / just under

## 动态图 第一句 + 第二句
- 句① 总体描述：动态**单**/多方向
- The chart above clearly depicts the trajectory of [主题词] from [年份] to [年份], throughout which it underwent a steady growth（上升）/ a gradual decline（下降）. (单方向)
	- with [单项] showing the most striking rise/fall. (多方向，方向不同)
	- 可选修饰词：steady、consistent、gradual、substantial、marked、rapid

- 句① 总体描述：动态**多方向** + 对比
- 一般只描述**两个变量**。
- As vividly demonstrated in the chart, [主题词] exhibited divergent trajectories between [年份] and [年份], with [单项] on a steady climb whereas [另一单项] suffered a continuous contraction.
	- divergent adj. 不同的
	- 如果两个变量**方向相同**，改几个词即可，例如divergent、whereas。

- 句② 细节描述
- Specifically, [Sth]  [nearly tripled / doubled / halved / 趋向动词 + 副词], climbing/dropping from [数值A] in [年份A] to [数值B] in [年份B], representing a gain/drop of  [增/减量]  [percentage points / people / units /  其余单位].（单事物）

- Specifically, while [Sth1] [趋向动词] from [数值] to [数值], [Sth2] [相反/对应趋向动词] from [数值] to [数值], followed by [Sth3] [现在分词短语] from [数值] to [数值].（多事物）


## 静态图 第一句 + 第二句
- 静态图无年份趋势，不用from...to...而是改为 in 年份；

- 句① 总体描述：静态
- The (pie chart / table) above clearly delineates the distribution of [主题词] in [年份], with [单项] accounting for the lion' s share.
	- delineates the distribution of **描绘了……的分布**

## 第五句、第六句、第七句（从主题词的好处的角度）
- 连接词（固定下来）：
- 第 1 点：Primarily, ... （首要的是 / 首先）
- 第 2 点：Furthermore, ... （进而言之 / 此外）
- 第 3 点（若写3点）：Equally significant, ... （同样重要的是 / 另一关键在于）

### 论点1 主语从句、同位语从句
- 任选其一，用于**写原因前凑字数**。

- 主语从句：It is widely acknowledged that ...
- 同位语从句：There is a growing consensus that ...
- 倒装+同位语：Hardly can anyone refute the fact that ...

- 本方案的重点和难点并非这半句话，而是省略号后面怎么去写——善用短语。

- 以下表达写原因时**通用**：
- 积极： serves as a primary driving force behind ... / pave the way for ... / exert a constructive impact on ... / produce far-reaching positive effects on ...
- 消极： exerts an adverse impact on ... / pose a threat to ...
- 密不可分：is inextricably linked to ... / is closely bound up with ...
- 基石前提： lay a solid foundation for ... / serve as the bedrock of ...

- **六大方面**，见下，写的时候**想不出来多看一下**。

### 论点2 not only, but also
- 公式：Not only does [主题词] enhance/elevate [方面 A], but it also fosters / facilitates [方面 B].

### 论点3 虚拟语气恐吓法（扣帽子、上高度）
- 主体可从**个人，社会，国家**角度。

- Were it not for [主题词], it would be virtually difficult for [主体] to achieve [某方面].

- If we fail to [do sth.], [某方面] would [be severely undermined].

- 「 **文字交个科社** 」  
    ( 文化 + 字[自]然/[自]己 + 交流 + 个人 + 科技 +  社会)
    心法：写大作文，使自己的文字成为一个科学社会主义论文！

### 论点4 比喻句
- 比喻对象：lighthouse/foundation/compass/engine/bridge

- 主题词 is as important as a lighthouse, providing guidance in difficult times.

- 主题词,  needless to say, is the engine that propels personal growth and social progress.

- 主题词, needless to say, are the bridge between [children] and [the natural world].

## 第八句 总结全文
- 开头：In a nutshell, / On the whole, / All in all,

- 基石/重要性型：The foregoing analysis makes it abundantly clear that [主题词] serves as an indispensable cornerstone of [某方面].
- 前述分析极其清晰地表明，[主题词]构成了[某方面]不可或缺的基石。

- 定调/必然性型：What the preceding discussion underscores is that [主题词] is no longer a matter of choice, but an imperative for [某方面].
- 前述讨论所深刻强调的是，[主题词]已不再是一种可有可无的选择，而是[某方面]的必然要求。

- At no point can we afford to lose sight of the pivotal role that [主题词] plays in shaping [某方面].
- 在任何时候，我们都承受不起忽视[主题词]在塑造[某方面]中所起关键作用的代价。
