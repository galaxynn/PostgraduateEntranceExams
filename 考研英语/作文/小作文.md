# 称呼与落款
- **称呼（顶格）：**
	- 情况 1：只知道**姓**
		- Dear Mr. Lee, （男）
		- Dear Ms. Lee, （女）
	- 情况 2：只知道**名**
		- Dear Paul/ John,
	- 情况 3：只知道**职位**
		- Dear Manager, （经理）
		- Dear President, （主席、校长）
		- Dear Editor, （编辑）
		- Dear Professor, （教授）
	- 情况 4：**啥也不知道**
		- Dear Sir or Madam,
		- Dear Friends,

- **落款（右下角）：**
	- 不区分称呼：Yours **truly**, / Yours,
		- 知姓名用 Yours **sincerely**,
		- 不知姓名用 Yours **faithfully**,
	- 署名：Zhang Wei / Li Ming

# 第一段（寒暄 / 自介 + 写作目的）
> **组合公式：** 【第1句：寒暄或自介（二选一）】 + 【第2句：写信目的（必写）】

- **第一句 A（给熟人：寒暄）：**
	- **主动写信（通用）：** I hope this letter finds you in good spirits.
	- **被动回信（答复）：** Thank you for your last (email / video / sharing).
		- 接情绪反应：I was so (glad / sorry / disappointed) to hear that ...
		- 接正面评价：I truly enjoyed it. / I was deeply impressed by it.

- **第一句 B（给生人 / 机构：万能自我介绍）：**
	- **个人身份（投诉/求助/申请/建议）：** I am a  student / regular customer / local resident) who (recently bought ... / cares deeply about ...).
	- **组织代表（邀请/通知/外联）：** I am a member of the university student union.

- **第二句（万能写信目的）：**
	- **主干：** I am writing this letter to ...
	- **词库替换（根据题干挂载）：**
		- **感谢：** **extend** my heartfelt **gratitude** for (your) ...
		- **祝贺：** **extend** my warm **congratulations** on (your) ...
		- **道歉：** **convey** my sincere **apology** for (not being able to ...)
		- **投诉：** **express** my **dissatisfaction** with ...
		- **建议：** **offer** some practical **suggestions** concerning ...
		- **邀请：** **invite** you to participate in ...
		- **咨询/求助：** inquire about ... / ask for your kind assistance with ...

- **知道型**：Upon learning that ..., I am delighted to write this letter to ...
# 第二段（逻辑骨架 + 功能插件）
> **组合公式：** 【引出过渡句】 + 【逻辑连接词】 + 【对应功能插件】

## 1. 逻辑骨架（串联词）
- **段首总起过渡（可选）：** The relevant (details / reasons / suggestions / issues) are as follows.
- **分点推进：**
	- **第一点：** To begin with, ...
		- **细节展开：** Specifically, ...
	- **第二点：** In addition, ... / What makes matters worse is that ...（仅用于负面/投诉递进）
	- **第三点（如有）：** Last but not least, ...
- **因果/总结：** Therefore, ... / Given the issues above, ...

## 2. 功能插件（按需挂载，后续逐步补充）
- **【插件1：提建议】**
	- I wonder if you could ..., so that you are able to ...
	- It is advisable for you to ..., which will enable you to ...
	- I would like to recommend that you (should) ...

- **【插件2：做投诉】**
	- **指出问题：** ... is far from my expectations. / ... fell far short of my expectations.
	- **恶化递进：** What makes matters worse is that (从句) / (名词短语).
	- **提出诉求：** Given the issues, I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter immediately and (issue a full refund / replace it with a new one).

- **【插件3：办活动 / 邀请 / 介绍 / 通知】**
	- **活动的基本信息：** The (activity / lecture / ceremony / competition) is scheduled to be held in (地点) at 8:00 a.m. on Sunday, December 20th.
	- **活动的目的**：The primary (objective / purpose / theme) of the event is to ...
	- **活动的流程环节：** A wide range of activities will be organized, ranging from (活动1) to (活动2).
	- **活动的好处：**

- **【插件4：表道歉 / 解释】**
	- **解释原因：** The main reason is that I have an unexpected (exam / appointment) at that time.
	- **补偿方案：** To make up for it, I wonder if we could reschedule our meeting to (新时间).

# 第三段（总结致意 + 盼复）
> **组合公式：** 【第1句：按信件诉求三选一】 + 【第2句：万能盼复】

- **第一句（按诉求选择）：**
	- **需对方行动（投诉 / 求助 / 邀请 / 申请）：**
		- I would appreciate it if you could take my (request / invitation / application) into favorable consideration.
		- Thank you for your time and attention to this matter.（尤适投诉/公务）
	- **给对方提供帮助（建议信 / 答复咨询）：**
		- I hope you will find these suggestions (information) helpful / useful.
	- **纯情感表达（感谢信 / 祝贺信）：**
		- Once again, please accept my sincere (gratitude / congratulations), and I wish you all the best.

- **第二句（万能收尾）：**
	- I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.
