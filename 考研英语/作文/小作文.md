	- Thanks for your (email / video / sharing).（不是答复信不写这个）
		- I am so <u>glad / sorry / disappointed</u> to hear that ...
		- I am truly enjoyed it.

- 第二句：
	- I am writing to ...
		- extend my heartfelt gratitude for (your) ...
		- extend my heartfelt congratulations on (your) ...

# 投诉信
Dear Sir or Madam / Mr. xxx ,

I am writing this letter to express my dissatisfaction with the 　（投诉对象，如产品或服务名称）　 which I 　（具体行为，如 bought / reserved + 时间或地点）　. Unfortunately, the experience turned out to be quite disappointing due to the following issues.

To begin with, 　<u>（投诉点一，填名词短语）</u>　 is far from satisfactory. Specifically, 　<u>（投诉细节一，填完整句子）</u>　. What makes matters worse is 　<u>（投诉点二，填名词短语或 that 从句）</u>　. 　<u>（投诉细节二，填完整句子）</u>　. Under these circumstances, I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter immediately and 　<u>（具体诉求，如 issue a full refund / replace it with a new one）</u>　.

Thank you for your time and attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely, 
Zhang Wei
