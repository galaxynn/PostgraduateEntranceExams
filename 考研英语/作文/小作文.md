
# 第一段
- 第一句：
	- 通用：
	- I hope this letter finds you <u>well / in good spirits</u>.

	- 答复 / 被动回信：
	- Thanks for your (email / video / sharing).（不是答复信不写这个）
		- I am so <u>glad / sorry / disappointed</u> to hear that ...
		- I am truly enjoyed it.
- 第二句：
	- I am writing to ...

	- extend 致以、表达
	- 感谢
		- **extend** my heartfelt **gratitude** for (your) ... 

	- 庆祝
		- **extend** my heartfelt **congratulations** on (your) ...
			
	- 投诉
		- **express** my **dissatisfaction** with ...

# 第二段
- 如果**想用连接词**：
	- 第一点：To begin with, ... 
	- 第二点：In addition,  ... 
	- 第三点(如有)：Last but not least, ...

	- Specifically, (细节展开) ... 

- 建议：
	- <u>投诉后的建议</u>，加 Given the issues, ...
	- I wonder if you can ..., so that you ...
	- It is advisable to ..., which can really help you ...

- 投诉：
	- ... is far from my expectation.


# 第三段
- 第一句：
	- I would appreciate it if you could ...
	- I hope you find this advice helpful.
	- Thanks for your attention to this matter.

- 第二句：
	- I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.


# 投诉信
Dear Sir or Madam / Mr. xxx ,

I am writing this letter to express my dissatisfaction with the 　<u>（投诉对象，如产品或服务名称）</u>　 which I 　<u>（具体行为，如 bought / reserved + 时间或地点）</u>　. Unfortunately, the experience turned out to be quite disappointing due to the following issues.

To begin with, 　<u>（投诉点一，填名词短语）</u>　 is far away from my expectation. Specifically, 　<u>（投诉细节一，填完整句子）</u>　. What makes matters worse is 　<u>（投诉点二，填名词短语或 that 从句）</u>　. 　<u>（投诉细节二，填完整句子）</u>　. Under these circumstances, I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter immediately and 　<u>（具体诉求，如 issue a full refund / replace it with a new one）</u>　.

Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely, 
Zhang Wei
