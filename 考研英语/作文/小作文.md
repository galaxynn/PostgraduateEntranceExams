## 2010
![[小作文 2010.png]]
- 感谢 + 邀请

- Dear Friends, 
- I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing this letter to express my gratitude for your warm reception and invite you to visit China.

- During the exchange program, it was your hospitality that enabled me to feel heartwarming in a foreign country, which greatly enhanced my enthusiasm. At the same time, the program allowed me to experience American culture, broadening my horizon. I wonder if you could come to China, so that you are able to have a deeper understanding of Chinese culture.

- I would appreciate it if you could visit China when you are available. I am looking forward to your early reply.

- Yours sincerely,
- Zhang Wei
