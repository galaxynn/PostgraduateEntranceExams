## 2010 感谢信 + 邀请信
![[小作文 2010.png]]
- 感谢 + 邀请

- Dear Friends, 
- I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing this letter to express my gratitude for your warm reception and invite you to visit China.

- During the exchange program, it was your hospitality that enabled me to feel warm in a foreign country, which greatly enhanced my enthusiasm. At the same time, the program allowed me to experience American culture, broadening my horizon. I wonder if you could come to China, so that you are able to have a different understanding of Chinese rich culture.

- I would appreciate it if you could visit China when you are available. I am looking forward to your early reply.

- Yours sincerely,
- Zhang Wei
## 2011 庆祝 + 建议信
![[小作文 2011.png]]
Dear Li Ming, 
I hope this letter finds you well. I want to congratulate you on being admitted into a great university in this letter.
Congratulations! What being admitted to a college means is that you obtain achievements in a stage, which is wonderful. Besides, it is advisable to search for the information about your university, so that you could have a better understanding of it. Universities provide students with numerous opportunities to explore career paths in the future, enabling us to achieve personal goals, so don't be afraid of making mistakes.
I hope these advice is helpful to you. I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.
Yours sincerely, 
Zhang Wei

## 2012 投诉信
![[小作文 2012.png]]
Dear Sir or Madam,
I am a consumer who bought an electronic dictionary from your online store. I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the electronic dictionary. 
To begin with, the quality of it is far from satisfactory. Specifically, Upon opening the device, it crashed suddenly. What makes matter worse is that every time I want to use its functions, it gets stuck. Given these issue, I would appreciate it if you could replace it with a new one. 
Thanks for your time and attention to this matter. I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.
Yours sincerely, 
Zhang Wei
