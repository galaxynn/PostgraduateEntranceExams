- Those who insist that the value of a university education should be measured solely by the starting salaries of its graduates tend to overlook the fact that the habits of mind it cultivates—such as the capacity to question received opinions, to weigh conflicting evidence, and to express complex ideas with clarity—are precisely what enable people to adapt to a world whose demands cannot be foreseen when they are still students.

- 那些坚称大学教育的价值只应该用毕业后的起点薪资来衡量的人，往往会忽视这样一个事实：大学教育培养的思维习惯——例如，质疑既定观点、权衡自相矛盾的证据和清晰表达复杂观点的能力——恰恰使得人们能够适应这样一个世界的关键：这个世界对人的要求，在他们还是学生时是无法预见的。
