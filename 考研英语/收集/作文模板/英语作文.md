## 14年大作文（柱状图--对比）

![a53f56e780b3149dba75ad4b53c1515f](img/a53f56e780b3149dba75ad4b53c1515f.jpg)

As vividly shown in the bar chart, China’s urban and rural population showed distinct changes between 1990 and 2010. In 1990, the rural population (834 million) far exceeded the urban population (300 million). By 2000, the rural population dropped to 807 million while the urban population rose to 458 million. In 2010, the rural population further decreased to 674 million, and the urban population increased to 666 million, narrowing the gap significantly.

This trend reflects China’s rapid urbanization over the 20-year period. On one hand, urban areas offered more job opportunities and better public services, attracting a large number of rural residents to migrate. On the other hand, industrialization and urban development accelerated the expansion of urban populations.

Looking ahead, urbanization is likely to continue, but efforts should be made to balance urban-rural development—such as improving rural infrastructure and public services. This will help reduce the urban-rural gap and promote more coordinated social progress.

Hope this trip suits your taste and that you all enjoy it.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 21年大作文（图表-柱状图）<练习>

![f1fa21d9e03dc16c0845465230e3e793](img/f1fa21d9e03dc16c0845465230e3e793.jpg)

As vividly illustrated in the bar chart, the survey on residents' exercise methods in a certain city reveals notable differences. Exercising alone stands out as the most prevalent choice, accounting for 54.3%. Exercising with friends follows closely at 47.7%, while exercising with family and engaging in group activities constitute 23.9% and 15.8% respectively.

This pattern can be attributed to two key drivers. For one thing, in today’s fast-paced life, exercising alone offers unparalleled flexibility in terms of time and location, enabling residents to seamlessly integrate workouts into their busy schedules. For another, exercising with friends not only serves as a means of physical fitness but also fosters social connections, making it a favored option. Exercising with family, though conducive to strengthening family bonds, is often constrained by conflicting timetables. Group activities, with their rigid arrangements, have a relatively low participation rate as they lack the convenience of other methods.

I, as a college student, deem that it is of great necessity for youngsters to do sports every day. It is doing sports that helps us to maintain physical and mental health.

译文：

正如图表生动所示，某城市居民锻炼方式的调查呈现出显著差异。独自锻炼是最普遍的选择，占比54.3%；与朋友一起锻炼紧随其后，占比47.7%；与家人一起锻炼和参与团体活动分别占23.9%和15.8%。

这一模式主要源于两个关键因素。一方面，在如今快节奏的生活中，独自锻炼在时间和地点上具有无可比拟的灵活性，使居民能够将锻炼无缝融入繁忙的日程。另一方面，与朋友一起锻炼不仅是强身健体的方式，还能促进社交联系，因此成为受欢迎的选择。与家人一起锻炼虽有助于增进家庭纽带，但常受时间冲突的限制。团体活动因安排较为固定，参与率相对较低，因为它缺乏其他锻炼方式的便利性。

作为一名大学生，我认为年轻人很有必要每天运动。正是运动帮助我们保持身体和心理的健康。

As vividly depicted in the line chart, China’s residents’ health literacy level (the proportion of people aged 15-69 with basic health literacy) saw a steady upward trend from 2012 to 2021: it started at 8.80% in 2012, gradually rose year by year, and reached 25.40% in 2021.

This continuous growth stems from two key factors. First, the government has actively promoted public health education through campaigns and media, raising residents’ awareness of health knowledge. Second, the popularization of the internet has made health information more accessible, enabling people to better understand and use health resources.

As a youngster who is preparing for the national entrance exam for postgraduate studies, I pay much attention to my health and I do sports regularly, which can bring me full energy, inspire my passion for life and help me improve efficiency in learning.

正如折线图所生动呈现的那样，2012 至 2021 年间，我国居民健康素养水平（即 15—69 岁人群具备基本健康素养的比例）呈现稳步上升趋势：该数据在 2012 年为 8.80%，此后逐年稳步攀升，到 2021 年已达到 25.40%。

这一持续增长态势源于两大关键因素。其一，政府通过开展各类宣传活动与媒体传播，积极推进全民健康科普教育，提升了居民的健康知识知晓率。其二，互联网的普及让健康信息触手可及，助力大众更好地理解和运用各类健康资源。

## 24年大作文（柱状图）

As vividly depicted in the bar chart, the survey on college students’ key gains from labor practice courses shows distinct results. Learning relevant knowledge ranks first, with 91.3% of students benefiting from it. Enhancing hands-on skills follows closely at 84.8%, while feeling emotionally refreshed accounts for 54.4%, and improving cooperation abilities makes up 32.6%.

This pattern aligns with the core goals of labor practice courses. For one thing, these courses are designed to deliver practical knowledge and skill training, explaining the high percentages for “learning knowledge” and “enhancing hands-on skills.” For another, individual-focused tasks in such courses may limit opportunities for collaborative work, leading to the lower share of “improving cooperation abilities.”

Looking ahead, labor practice courses could integrate more group-based tasks to boost students’ cooperation skills, while maintaining their strengths in knowledge and skill development. This would help the courses better foster well-rounded competencies for students.
